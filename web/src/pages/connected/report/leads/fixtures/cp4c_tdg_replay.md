## Findings

### Bottom line

Restructuring analysis is Not Applicable because no documented distress trigger is established; creditors should retain the distinction between elevated refinancing and junior-creditor dilution risk and an actual restructuring case.

The annual report states covenant compliance and sufficient anticipated funding, including USD 2,919 million of pro forma cash liquidity after the October 2024 distribution. Refinancing & LME favours ordinary consensual refinancing. Neither hypothetical earnings stress nor debt-funded shareholder distributions establishes a payment failure, failed financing or coercive exchange. Consequently, no restructuring path, reorganised enterprise value, fulcrum or class recovery is assigned.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 56; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; Refinancing & LME (CP-3C); Downside (CP-2A)

### Key findings

- **The distress gate is not met, rather than merely unquantified.** The filed covenant-compliance statement and management's funding assessment point away from a current enforcement or liquidity event. The reviewed evidence establishes no missed or likely missed payment, failed refinancing, distressed exchange, adviser engagement, creditor group or proceeding. Conditional acceleration language describes rights after a default, not evidence that default occurred.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 56; Refinancing & LME (CP-3C)

- **Stress is a warning pathway, not permission to invent a restructuring.** Downside's assumed 20% EBITDA decline produces 7.53x pro forma net leverage and 1.96x interest cover while cash generation remains positive. This describes demand weakness transmitting through earnings and interest coverage into poorer refinancing choices; it does not demonstrate a contractual breach or a failed funding transaction. The annual forward downside also retains positive free cash flow, albeit under assumptions.
Basis: Downside (CP-2A); Forward credit model (CP-2G)

- **Subordinated exposure is real, but a fulcrum designation would be false precision.** Legal & covenants and Refinancing & LME identify ordinary secured layering and payment subordination as junior-creditor loss channels. They establish exposure direction, not the marginal class receiving reorganised value. No valuation case or loss allocation is imported from the blocked peer comparison.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C); Peer benchmark (CP-1C)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest argument for preparing a restructuring now is that the shareholder distribution reduces the liquidity cushion just as a full-year interest burden approaches, while maintenance-light documents can delay creditor intervention. A commercial-demand decline could first compress earnings, then constrain unsecured market access before exhausting cash, leaving the earliest subordinated maturity exposed. That is a credible escalation mechanism, but the evidence currently supports ordinary refinancing rather than a distressed solution. A failed financing accompanied by an unfunded repayment need, a likely missed payment, an actual enforcement notice, a distressed exchange announcement or documented restructuring adviser engagement would change applicability. A leverage watch level alone would not.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Downside (CP-2A); Legal & covenants (CP-4); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C)

### Limitations

- The applicability conclusion is retrospective: the annual report was published after the assessment date. It supports the period-end compliance and funding assessment but does not establish what investors could verify contemporaneously or exclude an undisclosed distress development.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 61–62
- The absence of a demonstrated trigger does not certify the safety of any creditor class. These results establish applicability only; they cannot support a numerical loss estimate, an enforceable process choice or a claim that subordinated creditors are protected against future dilution.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C)

### Restructuring view

The prescribed exit is Not Applicable, not a blocked scenario awaiting a valuation assumption. The assessment date and issuer are resolved; no separate distress date, transaction scope or proceeding is evidenced. Incorporation and guarantor geography do not establish venue, governing law or recognition rights. Those legal decisions are not reached because there is no supported restructuring event to analyse. Claims reconciliation, path comparison, low/base/high valuation, waterfall construction and recovery-form allocation therefore remain inactive rather than being populated with ordinary-course balances masquerading as allowed claims.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C)
