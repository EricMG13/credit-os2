## Findings

### Bottom line

TransDigm's rating migration and nearest agency trigger are not established; creditors should treat the forecast improvement as conditional financial capacity, not evidence of upgrade headroom. The available rating indications concern facilities, come from a date-assigned testing transcription, and do not verify the issuer's rating or outlook.
Basis: TDG market quotes 2024-09-30.md, page 1; Forward credit model (CP-2G)

Financial policy is the clearest evidenced obstacle to durable improvement. The annual report explicitly places shareholder distributions ahead of debt prepayment and permits additional borrowing for distributions. Forecast gross leverage falls to 5.17x in the FY2027 base case but remains 6.47x in the downside; neither figure is an agency-adjusted ratio, and neither can be compared with an undocumented agency threshold.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–56; Forward credit model (CP-2G)

### Key findings

- **Financial-policy risk is observable, but an agency downgrade condition is not.** September secured borrowing coincided with the special-dividend declaration, demonstrating that earnings growth need not reduce creditor exposure. Further debt-funded distributions would first raise debt and interest, then weaken refinancing flexibility; the earliest observable warning is a new distribution or acquisition financing announcement, not a purported rating-test breach.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–56

- **Downside coverage deterioration precedes a demonstrated liquidity failure.** FY2025 downside coverage is 2.16x against 2.75x in the base case, while downside free cash flow remains positive at USD 614.4 million. An operational earnings shortfall therefore transmits first through fixed-charge protection and refinancing terms. These are investor monitoring signals, not published agency triggers.
Basis: Forward credit model (CP-2G)

- **Reported and supported earnings must not be relabelled agency earnings.** Financial foundation uses FY2024 reported EBITDA of USD 3,813 million; the issuer's EBITDA As Defined is USD 4,173 million; Earnings quality supports USD 3,871 million before unresolved items. Without agency adjustments to earnings, cash and obligations, even an accurately calculated investor ratio cannot establish agency headroom.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; Financial foundation (CP-1); Earnings quality (CP-1D)

- **The external rating indications cannot establish agency divergence.** The attributed Moody's and S&P facility labels have no accompanying action dates, criteria, outlooks or recovery assessments. Different agency symbols alone do not prove disagreement, and the facility labels cannot be transferred to the parent or to subordinated notes.
Basis: TDG market quotes 2024-09-30.md, page 1

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest counterargument is operating resilience: the forecast retains positive free cash flow in every case, and upside gross leverage reaches 4.62x in FY2027. The annual report also states that no term loan or note matures before November 2027, allowing time for earnings growth. Those facts support a potentially improving financial profile, not a rating outcome. A constructive migration conclusion would require dated agency evidence, an applicable criteria version, an issuer-specific upgrade condition and comparable agency-adjusted forecasts. Conversely, another debt-funded distribution or an earnings shortfall would weaken the financial profile before any agency action is established.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 56; Forward credit model (CP-2G)

### Limitations

- The only facility-rating labels are in a testing transcription with an assigned observation date. Consequently this assessment cannot affirm any rating as effective at the requested cutoff, even though secondary labels are visible.
- No agency-specific threshold, sustainability window or calculation definition accompanies those labels. Consequently the nearest trigger, signed rating headroom and the timing of any agency migration cannot be ranked or calculated.
- Annual forecasts use investor debt and EBITDA definitions. Their directional improvement and deterioration inform financial risk only; they do not justify classifying a case as inside or outside an agency's current rating range.
- No target instrument was selected, and the available facility identities do not all reconcile with the annual-report debt description. Consequently individual-security rating attribution and notching decisions remain withheld.
