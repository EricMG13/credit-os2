Scope and limits

What this forecast can and cannot show

  • Upside and downside are drivers only

    No leverage or cover path for either case. The downside carries one liquidity figure: the 2,354.40 shortfall at the FY2028 wall.

  • The base path is partial

    Missing values include FY2025 net leverage, FY2025 and FY2026 sales and EBITDA, and capex, cash interest and cover for each forecast year. Nothing is interpolated.

  • Thresholds not tested

    The 7.25x and 7.50x levels come from the filing summary. Legal & covenants: “Not assessable: no executed governing document available.”

  • Two leverage bases

    The forward model starts from 5.50x pro forma; the credit module states 5.55x pro forma net leverage.

  • Stress is point-in-time

    The downside module’s control, scenario 1 and severe results are one-period stresses, not forecast years, and sit apart from the three cases.
