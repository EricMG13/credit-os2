Bottom line

Earnings grew modestly but debt grew faster, so leverage rose and the room under both covenants narrowed. Calderwick still meets both tests with room to spare, and committed resources of 351 cover the 150 due in 2027. Show sources

Key findings

  • Revenue rose 5.0% to 1,932 and adjusted EBITDA rose 2.7% to 378; the margin slipped from 20.0% to 19.6%. Show sources
  • Total debt rose 124 to 1,890 while cash rose 41 to 151, so net debt rose 83 to 1,739 and net debt / EBITDA moved from 4.5x to 4.6x. Show sources
  • Cash interest rose 14 to 140, and EBITDA / cash interest fell from 2.9x to 2.7x. Show sources
  • Free cash flow fell from 102 to 72 after more interest and capital expenditure; adjusted EBITDA includes 56 of add-backs over reported 322. Show sources

Contrary evidence and what would change the view

Free cash flow stays positive, and the 2028 term loan B (640) and 2030 notes (700) fall outside the next year, so near-term refinancing pressure is limited. Show sources

The view would turn if adjusted EBITDA fell below 290 (the net debt / EBITDA limit of 6.0x) or below 280 (the interest cover limit of 2.0x), or if lenders did not accept the add-backs and EBITDA were 322 rather than 378. Show sources
