# Credit OS2 development coordinator
Revised operating specification · 27 November 2026 · pilot scope is software development in EricMG13/credit-os2. CP-OS continues to own paid issuer workflows. Project policy applies to the development coordinator in the initiating Claude or Codex session.

Use subscription access for development workers and reviewers. Use Astra at medium effort on

## The user contract

1. Type `/dev <outcome>` or “start the development loop”. The coordinator identifies repository, checkout, remote base, active spec, relevant rulings, current behavior and intended deliverable. If several projects or decisions fit, it names the ambiguity. Read-only research, design exploration and spec requests may end with their requested output. Ordinary questions do not start writers.
2. Before building, show one spec revision and its associated plan revision together. State the intended user outcome, scope, acceptance examples, known limitations, decisions already made, delivery slices, development time/spend ceiling, permitted model routes and who authorizes merge. Record explicit approval of that pair. “Explain D-04” is always read-only. Approval of an earlier version cannot authorize changed product meaning or a larger budget.
3. After approval, the coordinator applies its recommended reversible choices, dispatches only independent work, verifies, fixes and seeks owner authorization for merge. It answers side questions without interrupting independent work. A material change of scope, financial meaning, permission or ceiling pauses only dependent work until the owner approves the delta. A pause from the owner stops new dispatch and checkpoints active work.
4. Use the current project’s task reviewer, fix limits and independent integrated review. Give workers the approved example, relevant context, owned scope and return destination. One writer per slice. The coordinator alone updates shared records. Preserve required reviews, exact amounts and missing-versus-zero behavior. Provider availability is checked per client and environment.

## What the analyst sees

The chat is the first interface. Every update begins with decisions requiring the owner, then decisions already applied that the owner can redirect, then verified milestones. Each row gives an ID, a plain-English consequence, the choice made or recommended, urgency, and evidence. IDs are stable within the run and shown with its short name outside the thread. Reply in natural language.

The local dashboard reads these same decisions and drafts replies for the coordinator chat.

## Acceptance at the product boundary

For every analyst-visible change, including backend calculation, filtering, formatting, API and data changes, launch the actual application in a real browser on the candidate revision. Drive the affected journey with representative data. Assert the visible result and interaction, capture the rendered state and have Impeccable critique that state. Record browser/version, viewport, route, fixture, expected and observed result, revision, artifact and critique disposition. Required environments come from the approved support scope. Unavailable proof keeps the feature pending and can be routed to a proven environment. A unit test, screenshot of a mock, or a passing build cannot close this gate.

Grade a declared scope at a revision as Perfect for explicit H=0/M=0/L=0, Good for H=0/M=0/L>0, Bad for any H/M, otherwise Ungraded. Record first and final grades, first detector, reproducibility, fix, verifier and whether the fix reached merge. A branch update invalidates the integrated grade. Record later escapes against the shipped revision.

## Measuring and changing model routes

Collect events from the existing dispatch, review, checks and merge records. Each attempt has a run, task and attempt ID, role, task family and risk, spec/brief revision, client, requested/resolved model and effort, skill version, reviewer, candidate/merged SHA, timestamps and outcome. Record queue, active work, review, rework and human wait separately. Capture actual tokens and cost where available; mark unknowns explicitly. Keep raw source, prompts, personal data and credentials outside the metrics. The coordinator records decisions; an independent verifier confirms findings. Duplicate findings share one ID.

Evaluate builder quality by independently confirmed in-scope H/M per first-reviewed task, per-severity counts, verified closure, escaped defects and time/cost per accepted task. Evaluate reviewers by unique confirmed findings, misses on sealed holdouts and false positives. Compare within role, task family, risk, client and policy epoch. Investigate bad planning (wrong acceptance example), context (missing ruling), process (stale head, wait, tool failure) and implementation separately. A weak initial grade may mean the reviewer did its job.

Pilot defaults require owner approval with the plan. At ten eligible first reviews in a cohort, three or more tasks with implementation-attributable H/M trigger investigation. Compare incumbent and challenger on the same six withheld historical tasks, including clean controls, with identical context and blind scoring. Advance a builder challenger with at least two fewer affected tasks out of six, no challenger-only High, six accepted, independently verified outputs from each route under identical per-task attempt and time ceilings, unchanged gate coverage and median time to accepted work within 20% of incumbent. Require spend to fit the approved cap. Canary on five eligible low-risk tasks. Revert on a confirmed new High, exceeded ceiling or missing independent verification. Promotion also requires the five-task acceptance rule below. Apply it only within that project, client, environment, role, risk band and task family. Notify on trial start, switch, rollback or promotion with counts, time, cost, route and a decision ID. Wider access or budgets require approval. Thresholds are provisional operating rules, not statistical confidence claims.

## Authority, time and recovery

Use one coordinator-owned durable run record available to the executing client. Stamp each transition with run ID, event ID, decision revision, expected prior state and attempt ID. A single atomic conditional update accepts the transition once. The owner account and run must match. “Explain” has no state transition. A duplicate reply returns its previous result. A conflicting reply to an old decision shows the current choice. Bind each worker to the approved spec/plan pair and its dependencies. On redirect, pause or cancel affected writers, advance the decision revision and reject late results at integration. Reuse unaffected work after checking its assumptions. On restart reconcile leases, Git and open checks before retrying. If clients cannot share these semantics, keep one authoritative client for that run.

The approval card names a wall-clock deadline, per-attempt time limit, maximum parallel workers and monetary limit or approved unmetered route. Proposed pilot ceiling is four active workers per project, with one slot available for verification or recovery and at most two independent builders per feature. Atomically reserve headroom across concurrent runs before dispatch, including reviews, retries and model experiments. Reconcile actual usage once and release unused reservations. A missing price needs an approved upper bound or an explicitly authorized unmetered route. Expired or interrupted calls retain a conservative reservation until reconciled. At timeout cancel where supported, checkpoint the branch and try a bounded alternative only if capacity remains.

| Situation | Who acts and when |
|---|---|
| In-scope defect, stale proof, failed test, or worker timeout | Coordinator acts immediately. Verify the cause, retry or repair under the existing two substantive fix rounds, then invoke the project’s bounded escalation. Post an update when the outcome or deadline changes. |
| Routine choice with an approved default | Apply now. Include the alternative and reversal cost in the next milestone update. Show only occupied priority sections. |
| Product meaning, access, spend increase, exhausted recovery, or merge | Notify owner immediately with one recommended decision, the affected work and time remaining. Continue independent approved work. No reply leaves the question open. |
| Background improvement or duplicate Low | Queue in the existing record. Give it review time only when it changes the accepted outcome or during scheduled assessment. |

Before merge, require a fresh verifier for the declared scope, complete applicable checks and owner authorization against the candidate SHA and target base. Require proof that the browser served the candidate build, using a revision identifier and a fixture that distinguishes old and new behavior. Bind the approval to the tested integration tree. Re-evaluate on base movement or head change and verify the merged tree corresponds. Missing required proof gives an incomplete scope Ungraded for completion; any known H/M remains visible as Bad at detection. For a contract-only API change, verify its contract and record the evidence for a conclusion of no visible impact. Impeccable assesses the rendered experience; the independently specified examples establish numerical correctness.

## Route decisions in production

At task completion and on newly linked escapes, run a deterministic evaluation from the existing records. Deduplicate by event ID and distinct first-reviewed task. After ten new eligible cases in the same cohort, evaluate once per policy epoch/window. Report abandoned, unresolved, missing-identity and ungraded cases alongside the denominator. A changed reviewer, fixture, risk mix or plan revision that undermines comparability requires a fresh baseline. Missing or disputed attribution stays unknown/mixed. Record detection, diagnosis and verified closure separately. Missing outcomes on any of the last ten comparable starts hold automatic promotion until reconciled. Mark elapsed start-to-accept wall time separately from summed worker minutes. Parallel waits are counted once on the critical path. Attribute blocked time to planning, missing context, queue, provider, verification, rework or owner decision.

A blind scorer receives outputs in randomized order, a sealed oracle and the same resources. Failed, timed-out and abandoned attempts are outcomes. A failed candidate cannot earn a speed win by ending early. Freeze grades before revealing the routes. For the five-task builder canary require five completed independent first reviews, zero implementation-attributable H/M, zero known new High escapes, all applicable gates, total spend within cap, and median time to acceptance within 20% of the matched baseline. Stop on a failed criterion; hold promotion while any evidence is outstanding. Persist previous and chosen route, policy epoch, sample counts, expiry and override in the project dispatch registry. Atomically switch future eligible dispatch, check the resolved identity, and deliver one decision-ID notification through the coordinator thread. If the client cannot report the resolved route or guarantee enforcement, disable automatic promotion there. Honor an owner reversal across restarts. A later linked High escape rolls back a promoted route pending investigation. Keep at most one active experiment per cohort and require a new qualifying window before another trial.
