## Findings

### Bottom line
TransDigm's legal package is creditor-adverse: covenant-lite term loans and notes, a 6.75x leverage-based unlimited dividend test, uncapped cost-savings add-backs and Required Lender amendment power leave default risk driven by financial policy and secured recovery exposed to pari secured dilution. The only maintenance test, 7.50x net leverage, protects revolving lenders alone and was not triggered at year-end: revolver usage was 7.36% of commitments against a 40% trigger. Exact covenant headroom and first-lien enforcement control are not established because no compliance certificate, covenant EBITDA or intercreditor agreement is in the evidence.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 181; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 204–205; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–62; calculation K03

The September 2024 recapitalisation shows the capacity is used, not theoretical. Net leverage on reported EBITDA was 4.88x at year-end, 6.02x pro forma for the October 2024 dividend payment and unchanged at 4.88x for the October 2024 securitization draw; these are reference measures, not the covenant ratio, whose broader EBITDA would show more room.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation I01; calculation K11

### Key findings
- Maintenance protection is narrow, dormant and late. The credit agreement tests net leverage at 7.50x (8.00x for four quarters after a material acquisition) only when revolving exposure exceeds 40% of commitments, USD 364 million; year-end exposure was USD 67 million of letters of credit, USD 297 million below the trigger. A breach is a default for term lenders only after revolving lenders terminate commitments or accelerate, so term lenders and noteholders receive no early covenant signal and rely on payment and cross-acceleration defaults.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 191; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–62; calculation K01; calculation K02; calculation K05
- Distribution capacity is set by a leverage test, not a fixed or builder basket, and that test has already funded a recapitalisation. The credit agreement permits any restricted payment with no default and pro forma net leverage at or below 6.75x; the 2033 secured notes permit any restricted payment above a 2.0x fixed-charge cover, so the loan test binds. On reported EBITDA the post-dividend reference ratio is 6.02x, 0.73x inside the test, and 5.50x on issuer EBITDA As Defined, 1.25x inside; covenant EBITDA is wider still, so a repeat debt-funded distribution is legally plausible as earnings grow.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 181; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 75; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation K11; calculation K12

Financial covenant EBITDA adds back acquisition, integration, restructuring, financing and recapitalisation costs, incentive compensation, dividend equivalents and good-faith projected cost savings for 24 months after an action or acquisition, with no cap stated in the clauses read. Earnings quality already rejects USD 345 million of issuer add-backs, leaving USD 3,813 million against USD 4,173 million issuer-adjusted; the legal denominator sits above both, widening debt and dividend capacity precisely when acquisitions are being integrated.

The September amendment was conditioned on both ratios being met after the USD 1,500 million Tranche L. Secured noteholders need two-thirds to release substantially all collateral or adversely change the intercreditor agreement.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest counter-case is that capacity is ratio-governed rather than uncapped: pari secured debt stops at 5.00x, junior debt at 7.25x and unlimited dividends at 6.75x, each measured pro forma, and the September financing was conditioned on a financial officer certifying both incurrence ratios. Release of a material guarantor or of all or substantially all collateral needs every lender, secured noteholders hold a two-thirds veto over collateral release and adverse intercreditor changes, the issuer reports full covenant compliance, and the evidence shows no liability-management, non-pro-rata exchange or unrestricted-subsidiary transfer. That would win if a compliance certificate showed covenant ratios well inside the tests on modest add-backs, if the intercreditor agreement gave noteholders proportional enforcement control, or if text not read contained an express anti-subordination right. The view would harden on another debt-funded distribution, any unrestricted-subsidiary designation of an operating guarantor, or revolver usage approaching the 40% trigger.

### Limitations
- The first-lien intercreditor agreement is referenced but not supplied, so who controls collateral enforcement between term lenders and secured noteholders, any standstill and any purchase option are not established; the recovery-control conclusion for secured classes stays open rather than favourable or adverse.
- No compliance certificate or covenant EBITDA figure is available, so every headroom conclusion except the revolver usage trigger rests on reported-EBITDA proxies; because covenant EBITDA adds back more, those proxies understate remaining capacity, which makes the extraction and dilution findings conservative rather than overstated.
- Only the 2033 secured notes and 2027 subordinated notes indentures were read, and no security agreement or guarantor schedule is supplied, so collateral scope, excluded assets and perfection for the other secured and subordinated series are inferred only from the annual report's description; the recovery view is qualitative.
- The unanimous-consent list was read only for the clauses quoted; an anti-subordination protection elsewhere in the agreement would move the uptier finding from open to sealed.
- The guarantor-group income line does not reconcile with its sales and cost lines in the extract read, so non-guarantor value is measured on sales alone and the structural-subordination finding is sized on revenue, not earnings or assets.
- The revolver usage figure comes from the annual report signed after the as-of date, so the finding that the maintenance test was not triggered is a retrospective reading of the year-end position.

### Overall legal credit view
Overall, TransDigm presents an aggressive covenant package, scored 4 of 5 on a provisional basis, because term loans and notes carry no maintenance test, distribution and debt permissions are leverage-governed with thresholds the September recapitalisation shows the issuer will use, and covenant EBITDA admits uncapped projected cost savings. The main creditor protections are the 5.00x pari secured incurrence ceiling, all-lender consent for releasing material guarantors or substantially all collateral, the noteholders' two-thirds collateral veto and the contractual subordination of the 2027, January 2029 and May 2029 subordinated notes. The main weaknesses are the 6.75x unlimited dividend test, agent-executed pari security for permitted debt and the absence, in the clauses read, of an express bar on lien or payment subordination. The primary default-risk path is a further debt-funded distribution taking leverage toward 6.75x ahead of the 2028 and 2029 maturity concentration; the primary recovery path is pari secured incurrence diluting first-lien recovery. For security selection the decisive legal item is the unread intercreditor agreement. Legal & covenants completed with limitations; key gaps are the intercreditor agreement, compliance certificate and security documents.

### Nearest pressure point
The nearest pressure point is pari secured ratio debt at the 5.00x secured net leverage ceiling, because it is the route that funded half of the recapitalisation and directly dilutes every first-lien claim. The credit agreement allows same-priority secured debt while secured net leverage is at or below 5.00x and requires the agent to document the new lien without lender consent. A reported-EBITDA proxy places post-dividend secured net leverage at 4.62x, but the covenant denominator is larger, so actual room is wider than 0.38x. Each turn used raises first-lien claims against a fixed collateral pool, lowering secured recovery, while added interest weakens cover. Monitor new incremental or secured note issues, joinders to the intercreditor agreement and any further dividend declaration. A compliance certificate with covenant EBITDA and the secured debt calculation would tighten the view.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 59; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 205; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation K09; calculation K10

### Structural and intercreditor view
Value sits mainly in the guarantor group, which holds all secured and subordinated guarantees, but the notes are structurally subordinated to non-guarantor liabilities and the term loans' lien perimeter is described only at summary level. Secured notes rank equally in payment with the credit facilities and share collateral through the first-lien intercreditor agreement, which controls over the indenture in any conflict; noteholders are bound through an authorized representative whose identity and powers are not in the evidence. Funded first-lien principal is USD 19,702 million, of which secured notes are USD 11,000 million and term loans USD 8,702 million, so control rights matter more than ranking. Subordinated noteholders, USD 4,600 million, turn over distributions until senior debt is paid in cash and face payment blockage of up to 179 days. No recovery value is assigned here.
