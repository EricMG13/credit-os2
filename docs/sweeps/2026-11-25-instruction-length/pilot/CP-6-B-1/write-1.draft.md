## Findings

### Bottom line

Watchlist is the action bias: TransDigm's cash generation and maturity runway make near-term default unlikely, but secured-debt-funded distributions erode junior recovery and no authenticated price shows creditors are paid for that, so no position is supported yet.

Free cash flow was USD 1,880 million in FY2024 and pro forma cash liquidity is USD 2,919 million after the special dividend paid on 2024-10-18, with no term loan or note due before November 2027. Against that, the board declared USD 6,368 million of special distributions in FY2024, 3.39x free cash flow, and net leverage of 4.88x at year end is 6.02x pro forma for the 2024-10-18 payment. A 20% EBITDA decline lifts it to 7.53x while free cash flow stays positive, so losses would arrive through refinancing terms and subordination rather than a liquidity failure. Neither side wins the debate, and the portfolio posture is Requires More Work because pricing, executed debt documents and the fund's mandate are all missing.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); Liquidity (CP-2D); Downside (CP-2A); TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 56; calculation IC1

### Key findings

- Near-term default risk is low, and the bull wins that point on quantified evidence. FY2024 free cash flow of USD 1,880 million absorbs the scheduled step-up in interest to USD 1,524 million, the liquidity bridge to September 2025 ends positive at USD 3,550.1 million after all scheduled uses including the dividend, and in the stress case free cash flow reaches zero only after a 38.92% EBITDA decline. Lenders therefore face a refinancing and loss-severity question before FY2028, not a payment-default question.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Liquidity (CP-2D); Downside (CP-2A)

- Financial policy decides loss severity, and the bear wins it. The board ranks debt prepayment last, funded the September 2024 dividend largely with USD 3,000 million of new secured debt and bought back USD 316 million of stock in the following quarter. Each round adds senior claims without adding earnings: secured debt is now USD 19,534 million, 78.51% of carrying debt, ahead of USD 4,581 million of subordinated notes that can also be blocked from debt service after a senior default.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); Legal & covenants (CP-4); Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation IC2

- How much more secured, distribution-funded debt the documents allow is the single greatest uncertainty, and it is not established. The 5.00x secured net debt test gives illustrative room of about USD 3,244 million, and a repeat of the September issue would use 92.48% of it, but Evidence validation refused to clear that figure as contractual capacity because definitions, free-and-clear baskets and usage are not in the documents. The bear's legal attack therefore proves direction, not size.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Evidence validation (CP-5); calculation IC3

- Relative value cannot be judged, so no instrument can be preferred and no bias above Watchlist is supported. The only loan levels come from a transcription whose date was assigned for testing; the 247 bp discount margin on Tranche J is 54 bp wide of a StandardAero loan, but facility identities conflict with the filed terms and no bond spreads exist, so the gap cannot be attributed to credit.
Basis: Security selection (CP-3); Market-implied risk (CP-3D)

- The portfolio posture is Requires More Work, and the binding constraint is data quality: the fund's mandate, limits and exposures were not supplied, so the exact portfolio constraint is not established and sizing within the fund's limits is not tested.
Basis: Security selection (CP-3)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a higher bias is that nothing in the evidence points to distress. The annual report values the notes at quoted market prices close to principal, the issuer completed large refinancings and extensions in FY2024, about 77% of gross debt is fixed rate, and the term loans and notes carry no maintenance covenant that could trip. If authenticated prices showed the secured loans paying a premium over comparable aerospace credits, the decision rules would allow a starter position in secured debt even with legal leakage unresolved. The strongest case for a lower bias is that extraction is repeatable and accelerating: USD 6,368 million was declared in one year, buybacks resumed within weeks, and the swaps and cap fixing USD 3,400 million of floating debt expire by 2025-09-30, so the USD 57.89 million cost of a 100 bp rate rise on today's unhedged balance will grow. The view moves to a starter position for secured debt on dated executable prices showing compensation plus executed documents that keep secured debt inside the 5.00x test. It moves to Avoid for the subordinated notes if another debt-funded dividend is declared while EBITDA falls, if a secured basket outside the ratio exists, or if the securitization is not renewed by 2025-07-11 while revolver usage exceeds USD 364 million.
Basis: Security selection (CP-3); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C); Rates, FX & hedging (CP-2E); Business & governance (CP-1A); Legal & covenants (CP-4)

### Limitations

- The Watchlist bias is capped by missing authenticated prices rather than by credit evidence: priced evidence showing the secured loans paying a premium would raise the bias for secured debt to a starter position, while tight pricing would hold it at Watchlist or lower, so the bias is provisional on price.
- The bear's win on secured layering rests on direction rather than amount, because this debate declines to use the illustrative secured capacity as contractual; executed documents permitting secured debt outside the 5.00x test would move the subordinated notes from Watchlist to Avoid, and a tight cap would strengthen the secured-debt case.
- Chair scores cover seven of nine dimensions and are not averaged, so the finding that neither side wins rests on the resolution of individual disputed risks; a scored relative-value dimension could turn it into a bull or bear win.
- The governance judgement uses the board's documented conduct only, because who controls the board is not established; evidence of a controlling holder would change the expected persistence of the distribution policy, not the history the bear relies on.
- The portfolio posture cannot reach Include for any instrument while no mandate, holdings or exposure data exist; once they are supplied, the binding constraint could move from data quality to a concentration, rating-bucket or legal-recovery limit.

### Single greatest uncertainty

The single greatest uncertainty is how much further secured debt and how many further distributions the executed documents permit. It matters because the bear's mechanism, new secured debt raised alongside a dividend, enlarges the senior claim pool and thins the subordinated cushion without adding earnings, so its permitted size sets subordinated LGD and the dilution risk for secured holders. Resolving it requires the credit agreement definitions of consolidated secured net debt and EBITDA, the free-and-clear incremental and lien baskets, the restricted-payment baskets and builder provisions, and current usage. If the documents cap secured debt inside the 5.00x test with no basket outside it, the secured debt becomes a starter-position candidate once priced and the subordinated notes stay on Watchlist. If they permit secured debt outside the ratio or large restricted payments, the subordinated notes move to Avoid and the secured debt stays at Watchlist.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Evidence validation (CP-5); Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Committee challenge

A committee member should press two questions. The first is why not a starter position when every measure of default risk is benign. The answer is that a starter position with legal leakage unresolved requires relative value shown to be fair or cheap, and the only price evidence is a transcription with an assigned date and facility identities that conflict with the filed terms. The second is why not Avoid when the board turned FY2024 growth into USD 6,368 million of declared distributions. The answer is that the bear's stress chain ends in loss severity and refinancing cost rather than default, while the bull quantifies liquidity that outlasts the stress case, so the evidence does not justify exiting a credit whose compensation is simply unknown. The committee's real exposure sits in the subordinated layer, so any further work must price secured and subordinated debt separately rather than treat the structure as one credit. Evidence validation also blocked committee use of the legal-capacity conclusions, so the committee should not treat the illustrative secured room as a cap on future dilution.
Basis: Security selection (CP-3); Downside (CP-2A); Liquidity (CP-2D); Evidence validation (CP-5)
