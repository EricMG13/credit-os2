## Findings

### Bottom line

Watchlist survives the challenge: TransDigm's cash generation and maturity runway keep default risk low, but debt-funded distributions erode junior recovery and no verified price shows creditors are paid for it, so no position should be initiated yet.

Pro forma for the dividend paid on 2024-10-18, net leverage is 6.02x on reported EBITDA against 4.88x at year end before that payment, while FY2024 free cash flow of USD 1,880 million and pro forma liquidity of USD 2,919 million cover every scheduled use through September 2025. Against that, the board declared USD 6,368 million of special distributions in FY2024, 3.39x that year's free cash flow, and funded 69% of the September payment with new secured debt. The portfolio posture is Requires More Work because the fund's mandate and limits were not supplied and evidence validation withheld committee clearance on the legal-capacity reading.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Liquidity (CP-2D); Business & governance (CP-1A); Evidence validation (CP-5); TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–56; calculation C1; calculation C6

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against Watchlist is that the credit already looks sound enough to own. Reported EBITDA grew 21.12% in FY2024 on mostly organic growth, about 77% of gross debt is fixed rate, the term loans and notes carry no maintenance covenant that could force an early default, ordinary refinancing transactions of about USD 21,000 million were completed in the year, and filed note fair values show no broad distress. On the issuer's own measure pro forma net leverage is 5.50x. That case would win if authenticated pricing matched to the filed facilities showed secured spreads wide of rating-matched aerospace comparables, if FY2025 quarters held EBITDA growth near the nine-month pace of 24.30% with no new special dividend, and if the executed agreements showed secured and restricted-payment baskets tight enough to bound the next distribution. The first condition alone would move the bias to Starter Position for secured exposure; all three together would support Core Hold. The view would harden toward Avoid for the subordinated notes if a new secured-funded distribution were declared while EBITDA fell, if revolver usage passed USD 364 million, or if the securitization were not extended by 2025-07-11.

### Decision

Final Action Bias: Watchlist. The decision is driven by audited cash generation and a maturity runway to November 2027 set against a documented policy of debt-funded distributions, because the first keeps debt service internally funded while the second adds secured claims ahead of junior creditors without adding earnings, which implies a low probability of default but rising loss given default for subordinated holders and compensation that nobody has measured. The main factor preventing a higher-conviction recommendation is the absence of verified market pricing matched to the filed obligations, together with legal capacity that the executed documents have not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); Market-implied risk (CP-3D); Legal & covenants (CP-4)

That supports a Neutral — Stable credit implication for debt service capacity. The claim fails if trailing free cash flow falls below USD 1,513.96 million, the level that already absorbs the scheduled interest step-up.

### Bear cross-examination

The bear completes the stress chain to loss severity but not to payment default. Operating stress starts with a commercial aftermarket slowdown, already hinted at by the constructed fourth quarter, while scheduled interest rises to USD 1,524 million. A 20% fall in reported EBITDA takes free cash flow to USD 721.36 million, pro forma net leverage to 7.53x and interest cover to 1.96x. Unsecured markets can then close before the November 2027 subordinated maturity, making a secured takeout the natural route and enlarging the claim ahead of the remaining subordinated notes. The credit outcome is higher loss given default for subordinated holders, dilution for secured holders and refinancing on worse terms. The missing link for a default case is liquidity, because free cash flow stays positive until a 38.92% fall in EBITDA.
Basis: Downside (CP-2A); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C); Earnings changes (CP-1B)

The legal-control attack lands on structure and falls short on size. Lender control arrives late, because the term loans and notes carry no maintenance covenant and the revolver test springs only above USD 364 million of usage and loosens to 8.00x for four quarters after a material acquisition. EBITDA As Defined adds back USD 360 million, which enlarges every ratio-based basket. Incremental debt is permitted up to 7.25x net and 5.00x secured net leverage on the annual report's summary, distributions, investments, unrestricted subsidiaries, liens and asset sales are restricted only subject to unsized exceptions, and guarantees of the 2029 to 2033 secured notes follow credit-agreement guarantor status. Collateral release, voting thresholds and sacred rights are not established. The demonstrated exploit is ordinary secured layering, used for USD 3,000 million in September 2024; priming, drop-down and non-pro-rata exchanges remain theoretical.
Basis: Legal & covenants (CP-4); Refinancing & LME (CP-3C)

The bear's case is strong on recovery dilution and financial policy and weak on near-term default, so it caps conviction without supporting an exit.
Basis: Downside (CP-2A); Legal & covenants (CP-4); Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Single greatest uncertainty

The single greatest uncertainty is whether verified market compensation on each layer of the capital structure pays for the documented releveraging risk. It matters because the bull wins default probability and the bear wins loss severity, so price is the only variable that can turn a sound but extractive credit into a position: a secured spread that pays for dilution supports holding, while a subordinated spread that does not pay for a growing senior claim means expected loss exceeds carry. Resolving it needs dated, authenticated bid and offer levels for each term loan tranche and note, matched to filed identifiers and conventions and compared with rating- and maturity-matched aerospace comparables. A positive resolution, with secured spreads clearly wide of matched comparables, would move the bias to Starter Position for secured exposure, still capped there by unresolved legal leakage. A negative resolution, with spreads tight to comparables, would move the subordinated notes to Avoid and leave secured exposure on Watchlist.
Basis: Market-implied risk (CP-3D); Security selection (CP-3)

The compliance officer attacks each: the quote date was assigned for testing and the comparators are not matched on rating, guarantees or covenants; priority without a valuation cannot be turned into loss given default while the senior pool keeps growing; and no mandate, holdings, limits or executable size exist, while aerospace demand is a common factor across sector holdings.

The CIO scores downside, liquidity and legal protection on credit evidence and leaves the six market and portfolio dimensions unscored, so no average is computed and the weighting is provisional.

### Committee challenge

A committee member could argue that Watchlist is too cautious for a business that converts half its EBITDA into free cash flow and has nothing material to refinance for three years. The answer is that the debate does not dispute default risk; it disputes whether creditors are paid for a board that keeps converting growth into distributions funded partly by secured debt, and no verified price answers that. A second member could argue the opposite, that a bias above Avoid is generous when evidence validation blocked committee use. The answer is that the blocked items are the legal-capacity and ownership-control statements, which this debate excludes, while the audited cash-flow, liquidity and distribution facts it relies on were confirmed. The challenge that should change the decision is new evidence, not a different reading of the same evidence: authenticated pricing, executed documents or a further debt-funded distribution.
Basis: Evidence validation (CP-5); Financial foundation (CP-1); Market-implied risk (CP-3D)
