**Header**
1. Document: Exhibit 10.1 to the SEC filing (d496141dex101), an Amendment and Restatement Agreement dated as of February 28, 2013, among TransDigm Inc. (Borrower), TransDigm Group Incorporated (Holdings), the Subsidiary Guarantors, the Lenders listed on the signature pages, and Credit Suisse AG as Agent. It attaches Exhibit A (Amended and Restated Credit Agreement, $2,510,000,000, dated February 28, 2013, pp.62–303 of the extract) and Exhibit B (Amended and Restated Guarantee and Collateral Agreement, pp.254–333, including Perfection Certificate and Schedules I–IV).
2. Execution: Executed on its face. Conformed "/s/" signatures appear for the Borrower, Holdings, Subsidiary Guarantors (p.7), Agent, and lenders (pp.7–61). Many lender blocks have no second signature. No struck or inserted wording appears in the extracted text, so no page is shown as marked up.
3. Amends/annexes: Amends and restates the Credit Agreement dated December 6, 2010 (as amended March 25, 2011 and October 9, 2012) and the Credit Agreement dated February 14, 2011 (as amended February 15, 2012 and October 9, 2012) (p.1, p.65). Amends and restates the Guarantee and Collateral Agreement dated June 23, 2006, as amended December 6, 2010 and February 14, 2011 (p.3, p.254).
