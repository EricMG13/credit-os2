## Findings

### Bottom line
TransDigm's covenant package protects creditors mainly through incurrence ratio gates rather than maintenance tests, so leakage and priming capacity, not breach risk, drives legal credit risk; after the October 2024 distribution the secured-debt gate is the tightest constraint.

Term lenders and noteholders have no maintenance covenant, and the revolver test of 7.50x applies only above 40% usage, against 7.36% at year-end. On a principal-basis proxy using issuer EBITDA As Defined, net leverage is 4.39x at year-end and 5.43x after the distribution, against 6.75x for unlimited credit-agreement dividends; the secured notes allow unlimited dividends while fixed-charge cover exceeds 2.00x, proxied at 3.35x. Proxy secured net leverage rises to 4.26x against the 5.00x pari secured gate, leaving 0.74x. Recovery risk sits in pari secured capacity, structurally senior foreign-subsidiary debt and lien releases that follow the bank agent. The intercreditor agreement was not supplied, so enforcement control among first-lien classes is not established.

### Key findings
- **Maintenance protection is effectively absent for term lenders and bondholders.** The only financial maintenance covenant is a springing 7.50x net leverage test for the revolver, tested only when revolver exposure exceeds 40% of commitments (USD 364 million); exposure was USD 67 million of letters of credit at year-end. A breach is a default for term lenders only once revolving lenders terminate or accelerate, and only the required revolving lenders can amend it. Default would therefore come from payment failure or cross-acceleration, late in a downturn, rather than from an early covenant trip.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 191; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–62; calculation L01; calculation L02
- **Distribution capacity is ratio-based and has already been used at scale.** The credit agreement allows unlimited restricted payments at pro forma net leverage up to 6.75x with no default; the 2033 secured notes allow unlimited payments while fixed-charge cover exceeds 2.00x, a far looser gate. The USD 4,348 million October 2024 distribution moved the proxy from 4.39x to 5.43x, leaving 1.32x under the credit-agreement gate, so a further debt-funded payout of similar size remains documentarily possible. Exact remaining capacity is not established because covenant EBITDA and unrestricted cash were not certified.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 180–181; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation L06; calculation L07; calculation L08
- **Secured incurrence headroom is the nearest pressure point for first-lien recovery.** Pari secured ratio debt and incremental loans need secured net leverage at or below 5.00x; the 2033 notes permit ratio liens up to 5.25x. The proxy is 3.22x at year-end and 4.26x after the distribution, leaving 0.74x and 0.99x. The MFN protects existing term loans only against yield more than 50 bp higher, and for Tranche L it falls away for acquisition debt, debt incurred more than 18 months after the amendment took effect, and debt maturing more than 18 months after Tranche L.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 144–146; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 43; calculation L05; calculation L06; calculation L07
- **Definitions can enlarge every ratio basket.** Projected cost savings may be added up to 25% of post-adjustment covenant EBITDA under the credit agreement and 35% under the 2033 notes, equivalent to 33.33% and 53.85% of pre-savings EBITDA; on issuer EBITDA As Defined that is a ceiling of USD 1,391 million and USD 2,247 million. Dividend-equivalent payments and transaction and refinancing costs are also added back. No savings usage is disclosed, so this is a ceiling, not an observed inflation.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 24; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation L10
- **Structural subordination is material but bounded.** Guarantors produced USD 6,294 million of USD 7,940 million consolidated sales, so non-guarantors account for USD 1,646 million, or 20.73%. Both secured and subordinated notes are structurally subordinated to non-guarantor liabilities, and the credit agreement lets foreign restricted subsidiaries borrow up to the greater of USD 450 million and 18% of covenant EBITDA, which ranks ahead of group creditors on foreign value.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 57–59; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 60; calculation L09; calculation L11
- **Release and amendment mechanics favour the bank majority.** Required lenders can approve lien releases short of all or substantially all collateral, and the 2033 notes lien is released at the same time whenever the bank collateral agent releases, other than on a full discharge. Unanimous-affected-lender protections cover pricing, maturity, pro rata sharing, material guarantor release and all-collateral release, but the list reviewed has no express bar on subordinating the loans' lien or payment priority. An uptier by majority amendment is therefore theoretically open, not demonstrated. Noteholders need each holder's consent for ranking changes and two-thirds for all-collateral release or adverse intercreditor changes.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 204–207; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 117–118; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 136–137
- **Subordinated notes face a long standstill on payment.** Designated senior debt can block payments on the 2027 subordinated notes for up to 179 days in any 360-day period, with mandatory turnover of blocked distributions. This makes the subordinated class the first to lose cash in stress and puts it behind the whole secured class in a payment default.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest counterargument is that the documents are disciplined where it matters most for value transfer. Ratio debt under the main secured basket must be borrowed by loan parties, secured only on collateral and mature after the term loans. Unrestricted-subsidiary designation under the credit agreement is limited to newly acquired or newly formed subsidiaries, which blocks the simplest drop-down of existing operating companies. Changes to pro rata sharing need every lender, and non-pro-rata prepayments must be offered to all lenders of a class, which constrains selective exchanges. Covenant EBITDA is also likely higher than the proxy because the ratios are pro forma for acquisitions, so the headroom figures here are probably conservative. The view would soften if a compliance certificate showed materially lower covenant leverage, if the intercreditor agreement gave noteholders independent enforcement or release protection, or if the issuer retained cash rather than distributing it. It would harden if secured debt were raised toward the 5.00x gate, if a majority amendment subordinated existing loans, or if material assets moved to non-guarantor or unrestricted subsidiaries. The economic view also differs from the legal one: on reported EBITDA, Financial foundation shows year-end net leverage of 4.88x, rising to 6.02x pro forma for the October 2024 distribution, and Earnings quality treats recurring add-backs as not supported, so legal capacity can exceed what earnings support.

### Limitations
- Covenant ratios are analytical proxies, because no compliance certificate or covenant EBITDA calculation was supplied. This changes the headroom conclusions from measured to indicative: pro forma acquisition EBITDA and permitted savings could lower the true ratios and widen headroom, while restricted or encumbered cash would raise them.
- The intercreditor agreement among first-lien classes was not supplied. This changes the enforcement-control, standstill, turnover and voting conclusions for secured noteholders from findings to open questions; only the indenture's deference to that agreement is established.
- No basket-usage records were supplied. This changes every capacity figure from remaining capacity to a gross ceiling, so the leakage and priming conclusions describe what the documents allow, not what is still unused.
- The credit agreement was read from a conformed annex extracted from a marked-up amendment, where deleted and inserted text can run together. This changes reliance on provisions not cross-checked against the annual report; the key thresholds of 7.50x, 40%, 7.25x and 5.00x are cross-checked, while basket sizes rest on the annex alone.
- The security agreement, the guarantee and collateral agreement, and the mortgage and control-agreement suspension definitions were not reviewed. This keeps the collateral-release severity provisional, because the scope of automatic releases cannot be bounded.

### Nearest pressure point
The nearest pressure point is pari secured incurrence capacity, not a maintenance breach. Secured net leverage on the proxy is 4.26x after the October 2024 distribution against a 5.00x gate for pari secured ratio debt and incremental loans, and 5.25x for ratio liens under the 2033 notes. Until that gap closes, the borrower can add first-lien debt pari passu with existing term loans and secured notes, diluting first-lien recovery without any lender vote. Once it closes, further secured borrowing moves to fixed and grower baskets and to foreign-subsidiary debt, which is structurally senior on foreign value. Watch quarterly secured debt, new incremental joinders, intercreditor joinders and any covenant EBITDA disclosure. A compliance certificate showing covenant EBITDA and unrestricted cash is the evidence needed to tighten the view.

### Overall legal credit view
Overall, TransDigm presents an aggressive covenant package, scored four on a five-point scale on a provisional basis. The main creditor protections are first-lien security with an anti-layering lien covenant, constraints on ratio debt sitting outside loan parties, a narrow unrestricted-subsidiary designation right in the credit agreement, unanimity for pro rata sharing changes and payment subordination of the notes classes below first-lien debt. The main weaknesses are the absence of maintenance tests for term loans and notes, ratio-based unlimited distributions, large grower and percentage-of-assets investment baskets, generous cost-savings add-backs and collateral releases that follow the bank majority. The primary default-risk path is releveraging through permitted distributions and secured incurrence, so default would arrive as a payment or refinancing event rather than a covenant trip. The primary recovery-risk path is dilution by pari secured debt and value sitting in non-guarantor subsidiaries. For security selection the most important legal feature is the gap between first-lien and subordinated classes, sharpened by the payment blockage. Further analysis requires the intercreditor agreement, a compliance certificate, basket-usage records, the security agreement and the other note indentures. Legal & covenants is complete with limitations.
