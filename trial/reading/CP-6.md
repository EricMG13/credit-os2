# CP-6 reading list: TransDigm Group Incorporated (tdg), as of 2024-09-30

Case: TransDigm Group Incorporated, deep credit assessment as of 2024-09-30 (historical test case; anything dated after 2024-09-30 is a subsequent event). Remit: CP-6 is the committee debate and challenge. It tests the credit view the earlier modules formed (strongest case for and against, what the evidence does and does not support, what the committee may decide) and adds a portfolio posture, with the fund's mandate and limits declared not supplied. Files read: the remit head (`armA/CP-6.head.md`); `context.json`; the FY2024 10-K (`inputs/TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_…jsonl`, pages 19, 44, 52–64, 70–73, 120, 123, 145, 153, 172–174); the December 2024 10-Q (`inputs/TDG_2024-12-28_10-Q_…jsonl`, pages 4, 44, 46) for subsequent events only; and the upstream handoffs CP-0, CP-1, CP-1A, CP-1B, CP-1C, CP-2, CP-2A, CP-2D, CP-2E, CP-3, CP-3C, CP-3D, CP-4 and CP-5 (reader passages, statuses, and the registers cited below). The March and June 2024 10-Qs and the annual report PDF were not needed (CP-0 records the PDF as a duplicate of the 10-K). All amounts are USD millions unless stated. "10-K" below means the FY2024 Form 10-K jsonl; page numbers are its `page` values.

## Not covered

- Portfolio debate registers and the portfolio limits table: no mandate, exposure or limit was supplied, so the only solid expectation is that they are reported as not supplied (folded into CP-6-C5). No item tests the content of those registers.
- The dated decision and watch events table: the remit asks for sourced decision and review dates, but which events belong in CP-6's lane rather than an earlier module's is a routing rule, not something the evidence fixes; the dates themselves (2024-10-18, 2025-07-11, November 2027) are covered through F3, F5 and D1.
- Confidence inputs: counts of claims by lineage class depend on how each handoff words its claims; there is no single right answer from the evidence.
- Recovery, rating triggers and the forward model: Recovery & fulcrum, Ratings & triggers and Forward credit model are not among the handoffs listed for this reading, so no item expects a figure from them; C4 only rules out presenting such figures as established.
- The ownership-control point (no controlling shareholder asserted by Business & governance, challenged by the audit) is carried inside F1 rather than as its own item, because the 10-K only refers ownership to the proxy statement and neither side can be settled from the sources.
