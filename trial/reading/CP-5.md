# CP-5 reading list: TransDigm Group Incorporated (tdg), as of 2024-09-30

Case: TransDigm Group Incorporated, as of 2024-09-30, deep credit assessment; a historical test case, not a current credit view. CP-5 (Evidence validation) audits the other modules' handoffs against the supplied sources: whether material claims are sourced, calculations hold and figures agree across modules. It then states what a credit committee may rely on and what it may not. Files read for this list: the remit head (`armA/CP-5.head.md`), `context.json`, `MANIFEST.md`, the FY2024 10-K extraction (pages 4–5, 45, 52–56, 59, 61–65, 71, 73, 120–123, 144–145, 153, 171–172, 176), the 29 June 2024 10-Q (pages 3, 43–44, 49, 56), the 28 December 2024 10-Q (pages 4, 44, 46, 52), and the upstream handoffs CP-0, CP-1, CP-1A, CP-1B, CP-1C, CP-1D, CP-2, CP-2A, CP-2D, CP-2E, CP-2G, CP-2H, CP-3, CP-3C, CP-3D, CP-4, CP-4C and CP-DR (CP-1, CP-1D, CP-2, CP-2A, CP-2D, CP-2G and CP-4 reader passages and calculation tables in full; the others at their status lines, bottom lines and the passages cited below). Not opened: CP-5.md, CP-6.md, the annual report PDF, the 30 March 2024 10-Q, any method file. "Defect" below means something confirmed by reading both passages named in `CP-5.evidence.json`. Amounts are USD millions unless stated.

## Not covered

- Complete claim-by-claim lineage counts. The remit asks for counts of material claims by lineage class (Directly Sourced, Calculated and so on) and of findings by severity. These depend on which claims the module samples, so no single right count exists; the list tests the findings a sample should surface, not the totals.
- The support and contradiction map. Its edges follow from the findings above; no separate item is written because an edge list has no single correct form.
- CP-1A, CP-3C, CP-2H and CP-DR in depth. Only their status lines, bottom lines and the passages cited above were checked. No defect was looked for in their registers, so the list neither expects nor rules out findings there.
- The market quote sheet (`TDG market quotes 2024-09-30.md`). It is excluded from this bundle, so claims in CP-3, CP-3D and CP-2H that cite it (peer loan margins, sizes, identifiers) could not be checked against it. Only the five TransDigm quotes and two rating labels in `context.json` were visible.
- The annual report PDF and the 30 March 2024 10-Q were not read; upstream modules treat the PDF as a duplicate of the 10-K and do not rest figures on the March filing.
- Areas audited with no defect found: CP-1's income statement, cash-flow statement, balance sheet, debt instrument amounts and maturity ladder (every sampled line matched pages 120–123, 144–145 and 153); CP-1's ratio arithmetic; CP-1B's constructed fourth quarter (18.03% sales growth, 19.42% EBITDA As Defined growth) against the June 2024 10-Q; CP-2's calculations K1–K5 and business-mix figures; CP-2A's break points; CP-2D's bridge arithmetic and its December 2024 10-Q figures; CP-2E's hedge notionals; CP-4's guarantor shares and headroom arithmetic. One locator slip was seen and left out as immaterial: CP-2 cites page 5 for the 90% proprietary share, which is on page 4.
