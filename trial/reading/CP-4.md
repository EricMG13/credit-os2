# CP-4 reading list: TransDigm Group Incorporated (tdg), as of 2024-09-30

Case: TransDigm Group Incorporated, deep credit assessment as of 2024-09-30; a historical test case, not a current credit view, and anything dated after 2024-09-30 is a subsequent event. Remit: CP-4 (Legal & covenants) reads the executed governing debt documents and reports which document is operative, who the borrower and guarantors are, what the covenant tests and headroom are, what the main permissions allow, which creditors control amendments and enforcement, and what follows on default; it also supplies the covenant-headroom, legal-events, legal-entity and intercreditor tables for the report. Files read: `inputs/d887611dex101.htm.jsonl` (Amendment No. 17 with the amended credit agreement annexed), `inputs/d887611dex41.htm.jsonl` (indenture, 6.000% Senior Secured Notes due 2033), `inputs/d826058dex41.htm.jsonl` (indenture, 5.500% Senior Subordinated Notes due 2027), `inputs/d739389dex101.htm.jsonl` (2014 restatement), `inputs/d496141dex101.htm.jsonl` (2013 restatement), `inputs/d833756dex101.htm.jsonl` (Amendment No. 16), the 10-K for the year ended September 30, 2024, the 10-Q for the quarter ended December 28, 2024 (for subsequent events only), `context.json`, and the upstream handoffs CP-0, CP-1, CP-1A and CP-1D. ## Not covered

- Collateral scope, perfection and release mechanics: the Guarantee and Collateral Agreement and the security documents are not in the bundle, so no item tests what the collateral is beyond the equity-pledge limits in Section 5.11.
- Enforcement control between the lenders and the secured noteholders: the First Lien Intercreditor Agreement is not supplied. Only its existence and the limitation (CP-4-D2) are tested.
- Terms of the other eight note issues: only the 2033 secured and the 5.50% 2027 subordinated indentures are supplied. Whether the others match is not testable.
- Used basket capacity and dollar headroom under any permission: no compliance certificate, basket-use history or covenant-defined EBITDA is supplied, so no item expects a capacity figure.
- Recovery consequences in numbers: outside what the legal documents establish; only the ranking (CP-4-C4) is tested.
- The receivables securitization facility's own terms (the 10-K gives a $650 million limit and a July 11, 2025 maturity): its agreement is not supplied, and the only credit-agreement item on it (the securitization debt basket, greater of $725,000,000 and 20.0% of Consolidated EBITDA, page 60) was left out as secondary.
- Asset-sale, investment and unrestricted-subsidiary permissions (for example the $300,000,000 designated non-cash consideration limit and the investment baskets on pages 61 to 63) are real terms but were left out to keep the list to what moves a committee's view; a handoff that covers them is not wrong.
- The maturity date of the 2033 secured notes: the note form in the supplied indenture leaves the day blank ("[•], 2033"); January 15, 2033 comes only from the 10-K (page 60), so no item requires it from the indenture.
