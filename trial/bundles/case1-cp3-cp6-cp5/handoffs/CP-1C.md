## Findings

### Bottom line

Not assessable: no usable peer exists, because the research peer tables hold no candidate, no alignment observation and no peer figure, so creditors receive no peer-relative view of TransDigm's leverage, margins or valuation support for this run.

What can be stated is the borrower side of the comparison, on Financial foundation's definitions: a reported EBITDA margin of 48.02%, FFO to debt of 9.43%, total leverage of 6.53x and net leverage of 4.88x at year end, or 6.02x pro forma for the special dividend paid on 2024-10-18, with cash interest cover of 3.29x. Whether those figures are high or low for aerospace components is not established. A committee should not read the margin as best-in-class or the leverage as sector-normal on the strength of this work, and any relative-value or rating-peer argument elsewhere in the run lacks a peer anchor.

### Why the benchmark is blocked

The run settings name the research peer tables as the peer source. Those tables record a single placeholder row in each of the candidate, alignment and figure registers, stating that the issuer filings name no competitor and that public registrant lookups were refused. A search of the 2024 annual report for a disclosed peer group, a stock-performance comparison index or named competitors returned nothing. The method's fallback of web discovery could not run because no web retrieval is available in this run, and the source set holds no peer filing. With no peer, every peer statistic, outlier test and implied valuation fails its minimum-count gate, so none is stated.
Basis: Research questions (CP-DR); Source readiness (CP-0); TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl

### What the borrower figures would need to be compared on

The benchmark would turn on definitions before numbers. TransDigm's headline earnings measure, EBITDA As Defined, is USD 360 million above reported EBITDA in FY2024, mostly recurring non-cash stock and deferred compensation of USD 217 million. Any peer using a narrower adjusted measure would make TransDigm look less levered on its own measure than it is; this is why the borrower rows below use reported EBITDA as the conservative basis, with the issuer-adjusted figures shown beside them. Year-end cash also pre-funded the October dividend: net debt of USD 18,619 million becomes USD 22,967 million pro forma for the 2024-10-18 payment, so a peer comparison of net leverage at 30 September 2024 without that adjustment would flatter the borrower by more than one turn.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation B4; calculation B7; calculation B8; calculation B9; calculation B10

### Strongest contrary view

The strongest argument against blocking is that a creditor already knows the peer set from market convention and that TransDigm's margin is plainly far above typical industrial levels. That view would win only if peer filings were in the source set and aligned on period, EBITDA definition and perimeter; without them, any stated peer range would be unsourced, which the method forbids. The issuer's own research evidence cuts both ways: it describes fragmented, certification-protected niches, but its risk factors call the industry highly competitive and allow for price adjustments. Conviction on competitive position therefore rests on issuer characterisation, not on peer data.
Basis: Research questions (CP-DR)

### Downside and monitoring

The missing benchmark matters most in a specific path. If commercial aftermarket growth slows, as the research's derived fourth-quarter increments hint, while cash interest steps up after the September 2024 secured issuance, coverage would compress from 3.29x on reported EBITDA. Without peers, the committee cannot tell whether that compression leaves TransDigm inside or outside the range lenders accept for the sector, which is the question that governs refinancing of the FY2028 maturities. The observable triggers are any peer filing or rating-agency peer table entering the source set and the issuer's next quarterly margin and interest figures.
Basis: Research questions (CP-DR); Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation B6
