## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors are protected mainly by incurrence ratios rather than maintenance covenants, and the 5.00x secured net debt test is the binding brake on further secured debt ranking ahead of the subordinated notes.

On issuer-reported measures, pro forma secured net leverage after the 2024-10-18 dividend payment is about 4.22x. That leaves 0.78x of headroom, or roughly USD 3,244 million of indicative additional secured debt; on supported earnings the cushion shrinks to 0.45x. Pro forma net leverage of 5.50x sits below the 7.25x incurrence test and the 7.50x springing revolver test, which applies only above USD 364 million of revolver usage. Subordinated holders rank behind USD 19,534 million of secured debt, can be blocked from receiving debt service after a senior default, and rank structurally behind non-guarantor subsidiaries that produce about 20.73% of net sales. Restricted-payment baskets, the contractual EBITDA and the intercreditor terms are not established, because no executed document is in the source set.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 57–62; Financial foundation (CP-1); Earnings quality (CP-1D); calculation K3; calculation K5; calculation K13; calculation K15; calculation K18; calculation K20

### Key findings

- The package is maintenance-light, so covenants will not force an early default or an early creditor seat at the table. The term loans and indentures carry no maintenance covenant. The revolver test of 7.50x net debt to EBITDA As Defined applies only when usage exceeds 40% of commitments, USD 364 million, and it loosens to 8.00x for four quarters after a material acquisition. At year end the revolver carried only USD 67 million of letters of credit, so the test was not in force. Deterioration therefore reaches creditors through payment default or refinancing rather than a covenant trip, which lowers the near-term probability of default but delays lender control.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–62; calculation K5

- The secured incurrence test is the nearest pressure point and the main loss-given-default channel for subordinated holders. On issuer-reported measures, pro forma secured net debt is 4.22x against the 5.00x ceiling, or 4.34x if the securitization counts as secured debt. That implies 0.78x or 0.66x of headroom, about USD 3,244 million or USD 2,758 million of indicative secured capacity. On supported earnings the ratio is 4.55x, leaving 0.45x. In September 2024 the issuer added USD 3,000 million of secured debt to fund a dividend, so this route has already been used.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; Financial foundation (CP-1); Earnings quality (CP-1D); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation K13; calculation K14; calculation K15; calculation K16; calculation K17; calculation K18; calculation K20; calculation K21

- The total-leverage incurrence test leaves more room than the secured test, so it is not the binding constraint. Net leverage on EBITDA As Defined was 4.46x at year end and 5.50x pro forma for the 2024-10-18 payment, against the 7.25x incurrence ceiling. That is 1.75x of pro forma headroom, or about USD 7,287.25 million of indicative capacity. Most of that room can only be used as unsecured or subordinated debt once the secured ceiling binds, which caps priming but still allows dilution of all classes.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 62; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation K9; calculation K10; calculation K23

- The senior subordinated notes carry three separate layers of subordination, which is where recovery risk concentrates. They are contractually subordinated to all senior secured debt. Secured creditors can stop their debt service after an event of default. They are also structurally subordinated to the liabilities of non-guarantor subsidiaries. At USD 4,581 million carrying value, or 18.41% of debt, they sit beneath USD 19,534 million of secured claims that rank equally with each other.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 57–58; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 62; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation K24

- Guarantor coverage is broad but not complete. Value outside the guarantor group is the structural leak. The combined parent, issuer and guarantor group produced 79.27% of FY2024 net sales and held 78.57% of goodwill. That leaves USD 1,646 million of sales in non-guarantor subsidiaries, mainly foreign subsidiaries other than TransDigm UK. Domestic trade receivables back the securitization facility, not the credit agreement collateral. For the 2029 to 2033 secured notes, an entity guarantees only if it is a borrower or guarantor under the credit facilities or backs at least USD 200 million of capital-markets debt. A release under the credit agreement could therefore pass through to those notes.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 57–59; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 63; calculation K1; calculation K2; calculation K3; calculation K4

- Restricted-payment capacity is evidently large but cannot be measured. The covenants restrict distributions, investments, asset sales, liens and the creation of unrestricted subsidiaries only "subject to important exceptions". USD 6,368 million of special dividends was declared in FY2024 without any waiver disclosed. Leakage severity is therefore not established, and the main evidence of capacity is past use rather than basket wording.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest counterargument is that, for senior secured lenders, the package is protective. The term loans, revolver and six secured note series rank equally, share one guarantor group and a collateral pool that includes available cash, and the secured ceiling of 5.00x is well below the 7.25x total-leverage test. That gap should keep secured leverage in check and protect senior secured recovery. Nothing matures before November 2027, and the issuer reported compliance with all covenants at year end. On that reading the weak point is confined to the subordinated notes, and even there the summary shows no non-pro-rata exchange or drop-down. The view would soften if the executed agreements showed tight restricted-payment and investment baskets, no sizeable free-and-clear secured basket and sacred-right protection against subordination. It would harden if the documents showed secured debt baskets usable outside the 5.00x test, a builder basket sized off consolidated net income, or majority-lender power to release collateral or guarantors. Growth in EBITDA As Defined also enlarges every ratio-based basket, so better operating performance raises leakage capacity rather than creditor protection.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 58–62; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Limitations

- Every headroom figure is indicative, because the contractual definitions of net debt, secured net debt and consolidated EBITDA are not in the documents. If the agreements cap cash netting or count the securitization as secured debt, secured headroom falls toward the 0.66x case or lower, and the nearest pressure point moves closer.
- Basket wording for restricted payments, investments, unrestricted subsidiaries and free-and-clear debt is not established. The conclusion that the 5.00x secured test is the binding brake would fail if a basket permits secured debt outside that test.
- The intercreditor agreement the annual report refers to is not supplied, so the length of the payment blockage on the subordinated notes and the split of enforcement control between lenders and secured noteholders are not established. Recovery mechanics for those classes stay qualitative.
- The guarantor list exhibit is not supplied, so the non-guarantor share is measured on sales and goodwill of the combined group only. Entity-level value outside the guarantor group, including any unrestricted subsidiary, is not established.
- No refinancing and liability-management analysis was supplied for this run. The uptier and non-pro-rata exchange findings are therefore rated theoretical, not demonstrated.
- Governing law of the agreements is not stated in the annual report, so enforcement and guarantee-limitation questions in the United Kingdom and the United States are not assessed.

### Nearest pressure point

The nearest pressure point is the 5.00x consolidated secured net debt incurrence test, because the revolver test is not in force and no other maintenance test exists. The evidence is the annual report's statement that incremental term loans and revolver commitments require secured net debt of no more than 5.00x, against an indicative pro forma level of 4.22x. The risk mechanism is that the issuer can fund another distribution or acquisition with up to roughly USD 3,244 million of new secured debt ranking equally with existing senior secured claims, as it did with USD 3,000 million in September 2024. For subordinated holders this raises loss given default directly. For secured holders it dilutes recovery without any covenant default. The observable signal is any new secured issue or incremental term loan, and any special dividend declared alongside one. The evidence needed to tighten the view is the credit agreement's definitions of consolidated secured net debt and consolidated EBITDA, its free-and-clear incremental basket, and any secured basket that sits outside the ratio.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation K13; calculation K20

### Overall legal credit view

Overall, TransDigm presents an aggressive covenant package on summary evidence: there is no maintenance covenant beyond a springing revolver test, and the incurrence ceilings sit at 7.25x total and 5.00x secured net leverage. The main creditor protections are the equal-ranking senior secured package with one guarantor group, collateral that includes cash, and the secured ratio ceiling. The main weaknesses are restricted-payment and investment capacity that cannot be sized, guarantee coverage for the newer secured notes that depends on credit agreement guarantor status, and the triple subordination of the subordinated notes. The primary default risk is a refinancing event from 2027 rather than a covenant breach. The primary loss risk is more secured debt layered ahead of the subordinated notes. For security selection, the most important legal consideration is the gap between secured and subordinated claims, and the 5.00x test is what limits how wide it can grow. Further analysis requires the executed credit agreement, the guarantee and collateral agreement, the indentures, the intercreditor agreement and the guarantor list. Legal & covenants is completed with limitations, with a provisional aggressiveness score of 4 on a scale of 1 to 5.
