## Findings

### Bottom line
The sources support a retrospective fiscal-year credit assessment with material limitations, but not an unrestricted point-in-time investment decision: the annual report postdates the cutoff, the market sheet conflicts with the filed debt register, and the legal documents do not cover every outstanding instrument.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–59; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 116; TDG market quotes 2024-09-30.md, page 1

### Analytical read-through
Financial-period coverage is stronger than point-in-time coverage. The interim reports distinguish quarterly from cumulative results, and the annual report identifies annual statements, notes, debt terms and a cash-flow reconciliation of adjusted earnings. This supports detailed financial investigation without treating a management-adjusted measure as covenant earnings or proof of cash generation. Separate historical legal documents remain available rather than being discarded because later amendments exist.
Basis: TDG_2024-03-30_10-Q_0001260221-24-000046_tdg-20240330.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; TDG_2024-06-29_10-Q_0001260221-24-000069_tdg-20240629.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 80; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 71; d739389dex101.htm.jsonl, page 1; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 1

The immediate source-risk pathway is temporal contamination rather than a demonstrated operating deterioration. If later financing descriptions or the external quote sheet are treated as cutoff facts, the first failure is an incorrect instrument universe; that can distort interest, maturity and refinancing analysis before any investment comparison is made. The observable warning is that the quote sheet includes loan tranches absent from the annual report’s cutoff debt table and inconsistent terms for a tranche that is present.
Basis: TDG market quotes 2024-09-30.md, page 1; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 59

Only targeted source text was inspected; no visual verification or comprehensive clause review is asserted. An accessible agreement is not evidence that every cross-reference, schedule or security-perfection requirement has been established.
