## Findings

### Bottom line
TransDigm's supplied covenant package permits significant creditor dilution and shareholder extraction before lenders obtain a maintenance-covenant intervention right; secured status improves priority but does not establish control over enforcement or prevent permitted collateral releases.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 146–146; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 177–181; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 136–137

The principal weakness is the combination of conditional maintenance protection, ratio-based distributions and additional secured borrowing. The strongest protection is that core payment terms and specified sharing rights cannot simply be rewritten by an ordinary lender majority. Exact remaining capacity, current covenant headroom and an executable priming transaction are not established. The legal reading supports an adverse financial-policy overlay, not a finding of breach or a recovery percentage.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 204–207; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 116–117; Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Key findings
- **Maintenance protection arrives late and initially belongs to revolving lenders.** The ordinary ceiling is 7.50x, springing only when defined revolving exposure exceeds 40% of commitments. An acquisition-related ceiling can instead be 8.00x. Term lenders obtain the financial-covenant default only after the specified revolving-lender action; borrowing to absorb weaker cash generation can therefore precede a broader lender intervention.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 191–193
- **Distributions are not confined to a finite earnings builder.** The credit agreement permits any restricted payment with pro forma net leverage no greater than 6.75x and no default. The secured-note indenture separately permits any restricted payment with fixed-charge coverage strictly greater than 2.00x, subject to its conditions. These are simultaneous documentary constraints, not alternative permissions that can be added together.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 181; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76
- **The denominator can delay creditor intervention.** General credit-agreement projected savings are subject to a 25% post-adjustment cap and a 24-month action window, whereas the separate maintenance EBITDA definition contains no equivalent percentage cap in its savings clause. Earnings quality's rejection of recurring adjustments does not override contractual eligibility; equally, issuer-adjusted EBITDA is not a certified covenant denominator.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 23–27; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; Earnings quality (CP-1D)
- **Additional first-lien dilution is documented; selective priming is not.** Incremental facilities require total net leverage no greater than 7.25x and secured net leverage no greater than 5.00x. Permitted debt and lien provisions also support additional same-priority borrowing, while the amendment provisions retain sharing protections with expressly bounded all-lender offer exceptions. This supports dilution risk, not an assertion that an exclusionary uptier is presently executable.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 146; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 58–59; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 66; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 204–206

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest counterargument is substantive rather than reputational: the loan documents protect principal, interest, payment timing, specified pro rata sharing, material guarantees and substantially all collateral with affected-lender or unanimous consent, subject to their stated exceptions. Incremental debt also faces leverage, maturity and documentation conditions, and the general savings clause is capped. These protections make an unconstrained-priming description inappropriate. They would carry more weight if current certified ratios, complete collateral coverage and demonstrated retention of cash showed that the documented flexibility was unlikely to be used against creditors. A contractual restriction on further distributions or a tighter maintenance trigger would directly improve the legal assessment.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 143–146; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 204–207; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 24; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Limitations
- The distinction between maintenance EBITDA and general covenant EBITDA prevents a single financial ratio from establishing both maintenance cushion and transaction capacity. No tested bridge or eligible-cash reconciliation is supplied, so none of the legal thresholds below is presented as current headroom.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 26–29; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 81; Financial foundation (CP-1)
- The secured indenture expressly subordinates its security mechanics to intercreditor terms and forbids holders from directly enforcing security. Without the referenced intercreditor agreement, a first-lien label cannot establish which class directs a collateral sale, how proceeds are shared, or what release consent a dissenting holder retains.
Basis: d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 135
- Named parent, borrower and UK guarantor relationships do not establish the location of operating value or perfection asset by asset. Accordingly, the structural conclusion concerns documented claim access and release pathways, not a finding that a particular operating subsidiary holds stranded value or a complete entity-level waterfall.
Basis: d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 119–122; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 135–137; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 140; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Creditor implication
The nearest supportable legal pressure point is additional same-priority secured debt, not an identified imminent maintenance breach. The agreement supplies both an incremental mechanism and a separate permitted-debt route; additional claims can share collateral before the revolving-only maintenance covenant becomes relevant. Historical recapitalisation demonstrates that borrowing can fund distributions rather than productive assets. The observable retest is another incremental borrowing or secured-note issuance, with its proceeds application, certified leverage calculations and lien access. A complete assessment requires contemporaneous covenant calculations and the security and intercreditor documents; no amount of executable additional debt is established here.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 58–59; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 146; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Downside and creditor-control transmission
If operating cash generation weakens after the distribution, the first financial response could be greater revolving borrowing or another secured financing. More borrowing raises interest and competing secured claims; if defined revolving exposure then crosses the trigger, the maintenance test becomes relevant on its own earnings definition. A failed test initially empowers revolving lenders, and their specified action can transmit the default to term lenders. At that stage, the subordinated notes may also face contractual payment blockage after the required senior-debt notice, even though their payment obligation and default rights continue. Monitoring therefore needs exposure, covenant earnings and notices, rather than only consolidated financial leverage. This is a causal scenario, not an asserted breach or pending transaction.
Basis: d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 188; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, pages 191–193; d826058dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 106–109; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Intercreditor read-through
The subordinated indenture establishes contractual payment priority and turnover; those findings do not depend on assuming that unsecured means junior. Conversely, the secured indenture's common-collateral language and delegation to agents do not establish which first-lien constituency ultimately controls a remedy. A payment-blockage period is not automatically an enforcement standstill: the subordinated indenture preserves remedies subject to senior distribution rights. These distinctions prevent an overstated security benefit for secured notes and prevent treating the junior notes as having no acceleration rights at all.
Basis: d826058dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 106–109; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128; d887611dex41.htm.jsonl, page 135
