## Findings

### Bottom line
TransDigm’s earnings and cash generation improved, but distributions are the main creditor risk: the October dividend reverses the apparent year-end net deleveraging. Reported net leverage of 4.88x becomes 6.02x after the October 2024 distribution, holding annual EBITDA constant; gross leverage increased to 6.53x from 6.27x.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C09; calculation C10; calculation C21; calculation C22

The strongest counterweight is improved cash servicing: EBITDA cover of net cash interest rose to 3.29x from 2.71x, and operating cash flow less capital expenditure reached USD 1,880 million. That supports resilience, not automatic debt reduction, because acquisitions and shareholder payments compete for cash. This is a retrospective annual foundation, not evidence that the annual results were public at the cutoff.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C15; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–56; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Credit interpretation

Gross leverage worsened despite earnings growth because carrying debt expanded faster than reported EBITDA. Net leverage improved at the reporting date because cash accumulated before the declared distribution was paid. After the October 2024 payment, pro forma net debt is USD 22,967 million versus reported USD 18,619 million, and pro forma net leverage is 6.02x versus reported 4.88x. The reporting-date improvement therefore should not be read as a durable change in financial policy.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C09; calculation C10; calculation C21; calculation C22; calculation C35; calculation C41

Cash-flow quality improved: lower receivable and inventory cash absorption supported higher operating cash, while modest total capital spending left a larger cash surplus before acquisitions and shareholder payments. This supports interest servicing, but it does not demonstrate organic debt reduction because acquisition cash outflows and distributions consume that surplus. The combined stock and deferred compensation adjustment also cannot be treated as wholly equivalent to permanent cash savings merely because it is excluded from adjusted earnings.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C16; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 71; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 123; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 166

Liquidity has meaningful operating support and a distant major term-loan and note maturity wall, the strongest supported challenge to an extraction-led risk view. Yet the October payment removes a large part of immediately available cash, and the short-term receivables facility matures before the long-term-debt wall. Refinancing that facility or retaining more operating cash becomes more important if discretionary distributions continue. Net-interest coverage is favorable evidence, but an FFO-based debt-service conclusion is not established.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C21; calculation C25; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 59 and 62

Customer advances are a separate source of operating finance, not ordinary trade payables or incremental cash earnings. The increase in contract liabilities partly reflects acquisitions and advance receipts; fulfilling those prepaid obligations can require future cost without a matching new cash receipt. Contract assets also increased, so neither the advance balance nor its growth establishes a structural release of working capital. This matters when assessing how much operating cash can safely be distributed.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 142

The causal downside begins if customer collections slow or inventory must rebuild after the cash distribution. The first operational break would be renewed working-capital absorption, which reduces cash available for interest and acquisitions; reliance on revolving or receivables funding would then increase while debt remains elevated. Observable warning signs are renewed receivable and inventory cash outflows, lower operating cash conversion, and borrowing to fund further distributions ahead of the receivables-facility maturity. No unsupported stress magnitude or covenant headroom is inferred.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–57; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 62; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 123

The contrary case would prevail if improved operating cash generation is sustained and retained rather than recycled into acquisitions or distributions. That would make the earnings-led improvement in coverage a credible source of deleveraging rather than only debt-servicing resilience. Until retention is observable, the appropriate implementation is to separate operating improvement from financial-policy improvement, not to infer the latter from a pre-payment cash snapshot.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C15; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–56

### Limitations
This analysis uses the annual report retrospectively. It does not assert that the annual financial information was available on the assessment date, establish an executable financing cost, infer covenant capacity, or provide an independent security recommendation. The annual current/prior comparison does not reconstruct the supplied interim histories or the earlier annual comparative, and consequently does not establish intra-year seasonality or a fiscal-quarter sum check.
Basis: Source readiness (CP-0); TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 118 and 121–123

Pro forma event calculations isolate disclosed cash and borrowing movements, not actual October closing balances, and apply an unchanged annual earnings denominator. Neither the guarantor summary nor consolidated debt totals establish complete collateral priority, guarantee enforceability or the legal rank of leases, receivables funding and government advances.
