## Findings

### Bottom line
TransDigm combines a durable aerospace replacement-parts franchise with High governance risk for creditors: debt-funded distributions and acquisition priorities mean operating cash generation should not be assumed to produce sustained deleveraging. The risk assessment concerns documented issuer financial policy, not the qualities of individuals or an inferred private-equity sponsor.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 54

The decisive transaction is the September dividend recapitalisation: approximately USD 4,348 million was declared for distribution, backed by USD 3,000 million of new secured debt and cash, with payment after year-end. Proprietary products and aftermarket demand support earnings durability, but neither legally ring-fences cash for creditors nor offsets discretionary releveraging.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 63

### Key findings
- Replacement demand is the principal business protection, not immunity from aviation cycles. Management estimates proprietary products at approximately 90% of FY2024 sales and aftermarket at approximately 55%; certification costs and installed aircraft relationships support repeat sales. Reduced flying activity or a shift toward lower-margin OEM shipments would still weaken earnings available for debt service.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 4; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–12
- Cash allocation is structurally less protective than the operating franchise. The stated excess-cash hierarchy places capital spending, acquisitions and equity distributions ahead of debt retirement. The September recapitalisation required approximately USD 1,348 million of existing cash in addition to the new secured borrowing; this is a transaction-funding estimate, not a pro forma cash balance.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C1
- Acquisition appetite is demonstrated rather than inferred. FY2024 business acquisitions consumed approximately USD 2,347 million net of acquired cash, including a debt-supported CPI purchase. Acquired product breadth may strengthen the franchise, but purchase accounting and segment inclusion do not establish realized synergies or an acquisition-led deleveraging path.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 54; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 131–135
- Diversification moderates individual-customer risk but leaves common aerospace exposures. The top ten customers represented approximately 42% of FY2024 sales, while no individual customer exceeded 10%. Defense represented approximately 40%, creating a counterweight to commercial aviation but retaining procurement, audit and budget-timing risks.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest counterargument is that the business has repeat aftermarket demand, relatively modest physical capital spending, demonstrated refinancing access and audited public reporting. These support debt-service resilience and make an opacity-driven governance conclusion inappropriate. They do not cancel the observed transfer of cash to equity. The financial-policy view would improve with sustained debt reduction, distributions explicitly subordinated to balance-sheet repair and evidence that acquisition returns are being converted into retained cash rather than new distributions. A deterioration in aftermarket demand concurrent with another distribution would instead strengthen the concern.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 4; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 53–54; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 118; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 164

### Limitations
- Extraction can be attributed to the issuer's board-authorized financial policy, but not to a particular controlling shareholder. The reviewed disclosures do not establish beneficial voting stakes or special consent rights; this prevents a holder-specific control or exit-pressure conclusion.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, pages 39–39; TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 79
- The historical recapitalisation establishes willingness to distribute, not permission for another transaction. Without a legal-capacity assessment and current compliance evidence, future extraction size and feasibility are not established; no dividend headroom or priming propensity is inferred.
Basis: TDG_2024-09-30_10-K_0001260221-24-000083_tdg-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 63; d887611dex101.htm.jsonl, page 1
- Acquisition-led earnings durability remains conditional on delivery, not purchase accounting. Acquisition-level realized returns, integration savings and full-year cash conversion are not established, so the acquisition assessment is Mixed rather than a finding of either value creation or destruction.

### Downside transmission and monitoring
A slowdown in commercial flying would first affect aftermarket replacement demand; a lower aftermarket mix would then pressure margins as well as sales. If customer inventory adjustments or supplier certification delays accompanied that slowdown, cash receipts could lag production spending. Because distributions and acquisitions compete with debt repayment, less retained cash would leave the company more reliant on continuing operating recovery or refinancing. Observable warnings are weaker aftermarket orders and shipments, adverse channel mix, contract-asset growth without collections, or another distribution financed by borrowing. No numerical stress or default probability is established by these business facts.
